FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new investigation?

From the main menu, tap New Game to begin a fresh investigation from Case 1. If you've already started, you can use Continue to resume where you left off, or navigate to the Case Selection screen to replay a specific case.

How do I talk to witnesses?

Walk up to any dog character in the city and tap the speech bubble icon that appears above them. Each witness has unique dialogue and may give you hints if you talk to them multiple times throughout the investigation.

How do I collect clues and evidence?

When you're near an interactable object or clue, an Investigate button will appear on-screen. Tap it to examine the item. Collected clues are stored in your Backpack, which you can open at any time from the HUD.

I'm stuck on a case — any hints?

Try re-talking to every witness you've already met — they sometimes give new information after you've found certain clues. Also check your Backpack to review all collected evidence. Pay attention to what's marked on the minimap, as it highlights points of interest.

How do I control my character?

Use the on-screen joystick on the left side of the screen to move your character around the city. Interaction buttons (investigate, talk) appear automatically when you are near something you can interact with.

Can I save my progress?

Yes! Dogtective automatically saves your progress as you play. You can also tap the Save button in the pause menu to create a manual save at any point.

How do I turn off music or haptics?

Open the Pause menu (tap the pause button in the top-right corner during gameplay). From there you can toggle music and haptic feedback on or off independently.

The game is crashing or won't load — what should I do?

First, try closing the app completely and reopening it. If the issue persists:

  • Make sure your device is running the latest version of iOS/iPadOS.
  • Check that you have enough free storage on your device.
  • Try restarting your device.
  • Delete and reinstall the app (your progress is saved in iCloud if enabled).

If none of these help, please contact us with your device model and iOS version.

How many cases are in Dogtective?

The current version of Dogtective features multiple cases to solve, with more being added in future updates. Stay tuned — more mysteries are on the way!

Is Dogtective free to play?

Check the App Store listing for the current pricing and available in-app purchases. We aim to keep the core mystery experience fully accessible.

Getting Started

How to play

1

Choose a Case

Pick a mystery from the case board. Each case is a self-contained story with unique suspects and locations.

2

Explore & Investigate

Roam the city freely. Approach objects and characters to interact. Use the minimap to find points of interest.

3

Question Everyone

Talk to every dog you meet. Witnesses, bystanders, and suspects all hold pieces of the puzzle.

4

Crack the Case

Gather enough evidence, connect the clues in your backpack, and reveal the truth behind the crime.
