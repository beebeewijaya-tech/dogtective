The Mystery Awaits

A city full of dogs.
A crime left unsolved.

In Dogtective, you'll investigate crimes, uncover secrets, and solve mysteries hidden throughout a lively city full of dogs. Search crime scenes, question witnesses, discover clues, and connect the evidence to reveal the truth.

Whether you're a mystery fan or simply looking for a charming adventure, Dogtective offers a fun and relaxing experience packed with stories, puzzles, and surprises.
